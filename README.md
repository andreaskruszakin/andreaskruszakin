### Andreas Kruszakin-Liboska

A system with a feeling.

I'm Andreas, a growth-focused design engineer who is design first and builds the system underneath. I make founders' products easy to get, so customers buy and investors say yes, which so far has meant £1.4M raised for Neos and $172M for Schneider Electric.

Right now I'm a Senior Product Design Engineer at [Cal.com](https://cal.com), working on [coss ui](https://coss.com/ui), accessibility, and the production UI that sits on top of them. Based in Amsterdam.

#### What I build

- **Cheval.** My design system. Tokens, 22 themes, icons, components and motion, and every project I start pulls from it.
- **Design Studios.** Prototyping playgrounds where I design each screen in code, crit it against written principles, and pick from variants that disagree with each other.
- **Sites and products for founders.** Early-stage teams who need a customer or an investor to understand the product on the first screen.
- **Motion studies.** Shaders, generative grids, WebGL card scenes, and the occasional animated horse.
